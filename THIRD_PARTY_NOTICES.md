## ISCC–NBS centroid colors

`src/lib/iscc-nbs.ts` contains the ISCC–NBS centroid color names and sRGB values from the NBS/ISCC Color System dictionary (http://tx4.us/nbs-iscc.htm), as published at https://people.csail.mit.edu/jaffer/Color/nbs-iscc.txt. Names are lowercased and underscores replaced with spaces.
